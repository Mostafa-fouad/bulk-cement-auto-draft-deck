The Problem

Today, pricing Bulk Cement is manual and repetitive

One Procurement Manager per province runs the same loop every single day:

  1. 1 Calls manufacturers to collect the day's Bulk Cement prices
  2. 2 Enters them into a spreadsheet, per factory customer
  3. 3 Calculates the buy price and selling price by hand
  4. 4 Re-types those prices into every Bulk RFQ, one by one
this is the bit
that kills the day
Why It Hurts

The hidden cost of the daily sheet

Slow to respond

Every RFQ waits for manual copy-paste, delaying quotes to factory customers.

Error-prone

Typing prices by hand into each RFQ invites mistakes and mispriced deals.

Not scalable

Knowledge lives in one person's sheet — hard to cover, audit, or scale per province.

The Solution · In a Nutshell

Prices flow from the supplier portfolio straight into a draft

The manager still owns the day's prices — but instead of re-typing them into every RFQ, the system reads the latest portfolio price per product and auto-creates a draft quotation with supplier & credit price already filled in.

we don't price for them — we just stop the re-typing

Look up by SKU

For each RFQ line we fetch the freshest supplier-portfolio price in the province.

Draft, pre-filled

A draft quotation is created automatically with the credit price from the portfolio.

Clear signal

A flag tells the UI these prices came from the portfolio, shown via a tooltip.

Safe Rollout

Shipped behind a feature flag

The whole auto-draft flow is gated at the start, so it stays off until we're ready — and can be switched on province-by-province without a redeploy.

Off by default

Flag enable_portfolio_pricing_auto_draft guards the flow before any lookup or draft is created.

Never blocks a raise

Auto-draft runs async and best-effort; failures are logged, the RFQ raise is untouched.
